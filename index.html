Inner Mongolia, China · Cashmere & Silk

Cashmere, in its quietest and warmest form.

Bovo makes 100% cashmere scarves and silk quilts. Our company is based in Inner Mongolia, China, where the fibre we work with is prized for its softness and warmth. We sell our products on Amazon Japan, where customers outside China can find them.

Our Products About Bovo

What we do

A small textile business, focused on the fibre.

We have spent more than a decade producing and selling cashmere products. Our work covers cashmere scarves and accessories, and silk quilts. We sell primarily through e-commerce, including Amazon Japan, and also through our own store on 1688, a Chinese B2B marketplace.

01

100% cashmere scarves

Solid-colour, reversible, checked and patterned scarves, produced for cold-weather wear. All of the scarves shown on this site are our own products.

02

Cashmere accessories

Smaller pieces that use cashmere in the same way as our scarves, including triangle scarves and a reversible lace-knitted scarf.

03

Silk quilts

Silk-filled quilts in a range of sizes, sold alongside our cashmere line as part of our textile business.

Our fibre

Cashmere sourced from Alxa, Inner Mongolia.

Our company is located in Inner Mongolia, China. The cashmere we use comes from Alxa, in western Inner Mongolia, an area known for its fine, light cashmere. We work this fibre into scarves for cold-weather wear and sell them through our online channels, including Amazon Japan.

Bovo is a Chinese textile company and is not located in Alxa itself. To read more about our company and our experience, see About Us.
